Lemon Pasta in 15 Minutes

This simple pasta uses only a handful of ingredients: spaghetti, lemons, butter, parmesan and black pepper.

Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 1 lemon (zest and juice)
  • 30 g butter
  • 40 g parmesan
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water.
  2. Melt the butter with the lemon zest.
  3. Toss everything with a splash of pasta water and the cheese.
